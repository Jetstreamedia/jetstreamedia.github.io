CTR, CPL and ROAS: Meta Ads metrics explained simply

Nov 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Open Ads Manager and you will see dozens of columns. You do not need all of them. Start with three numbers, and learn what each one is telling you.

CTR: is the ad interesting?

Click-through rate is clicks divided by impressions. It shows how many people who saw your ad cared enough to click. A low CTR usually means the creative is weak or the audience is wrong. Before you change anything else, test new images, videos and opening lines.

CPL: what does one enquiry cost?

Cost per lead is your spend divided by the number of leads. Compare it with what a customer is worth to you, not with other businesses. If CTR is healthy but CPL is high, the problem is often after the click: a slow landing page, a long form or an unclear offer.

ROAS: is the money coming back?

Return on ad spend is revenue divided by ad spend. A ROAS of 2 means every 1 you spend brings back 2 in revenue. Revenue is not profit, so check your margin. A simple rule: break-even ROAS equals 1 divided by your profit margin. With a 40% margin, you need a ROAS of 2.5 just to break even.

Reading them together

  • Low CTR: fix the creative and the audience first.
  • Good CTR, high CPL: look at the landing page, form and offer.
  • Fine CPL, low ROAS: check lead quality and how fast you follow up.

A simple habit

Look at these three numbers every few days, change one thing at a time, and write down what you changed. After a few weeks you will know what works for your business.
