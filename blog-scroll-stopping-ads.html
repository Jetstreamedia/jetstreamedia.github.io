5 things that make an ad creative stop the scroll

Nov 5, 2026 · 3 min read

Most people see your ad while moving their thumb. You have about a second to give them a reason to stop. These five habits help.

1. Show the product or the result first

Do not make people wait. Put the product, or the outcome it creates, in the first frame or at the centre of the image.

2. One ad, one message

An ad that tries to say everything says nothing. Pick one benefit, one offer and one audience, and build the ad around it.

3. Design for the phone

Use big, short text with strong contrast. If it is hard to read on a small screen at arm's length, it will be skipped.

4. Make it feel native

UGC-style content, with real people using the product in a natural setting, often fits the feed better than a polished studio ad. It feels like a recommendation, not an interruption.

5. Tell people what to do next

End with one clear step: shop now, send a message, book a call. A good ad that does not ask for action wastes the attention it earned.

Test one thing at a time

Change the opening line, the image or the offer, but not all at once. Then you will know which change made the difference.
